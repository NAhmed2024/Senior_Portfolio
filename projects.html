Heart Hunter Highway

In this game, you clear a road of negativity, such as selfish, distracted drivers and general bad-mood honkers, using your powers so the road is safe for everyone. Bad drivers, especially negligent ones who are on their phones or don't care about civilians, cause a lot of unfair deaths and destruction. In this game, you remove them from the road and add positive feelings so that everyone is safe and happy.
